## 🎙️ MASTER SPEECH-CRAFT TIPS

### Pacing
- Average speaker says ~150 words per minute
- This script is calibrated to ~140 wpm to give breathing room
- **If you go over time, drop slides 13 (Leadership) or 14 (Roadmap)** — keep slides 1, 5, 6, 10, 11, 15

### Bilingual delivery
- Open in **English**, sprinkle BM phrases throughout
- If a panellist asks in BM, reply in BM
- The opening and closing — say at least one full sentence in BM
- The phrase **"UTM Sanjungan Bangsa"** is non-negotiable — say it on Slide 11

### Three signature lines (memorise verbatim)
1. *"Zero accidents. Zero hunger. Zero left behind."* (Slide 4)
2. *"Alignment is not coincidence; it is design."* (Slide 12)
3. *"Whatever the outcome, UTM can count on me."* (Slide 15)

### When you forget your line
- Pause. Smile. Look at the slide.
- Read the eyebrow / heading aloud — it brings the script back.
- A 2-second pause is fine; a panicked filler is not.

### Eye contact rotation
- During each slide, briefly meet eyes with each panellist
- Land your strongest sentence while looking at the *senior-most* panellist (usually centre)

### The "ADAB" cue
- Begin with *Assalamualaikum* or *Salam Sejahtera*
- Address the panel as *Yang Berbahagia* / *Tuan-Tuan dan Puan-Puan*
- Never interrupt a question
- Always reply: "*Terima kasih atas soalan ini, Tuan / Puan…*" before answering
